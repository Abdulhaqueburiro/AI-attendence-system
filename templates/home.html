How it Works

From check-in to dashboard, in five steps.

Each lecture follows the same simple flow — built so attendance can't be faked, and no one has to manage a register by hand.

Register & Enroll
Student creates an account and enrolls their face — a one-time, few-second setup.
Teacher Starts Lecture
A unique check-in link is generated and shared with the class.
Scan & Verify
Student opens the link and scans their face — a natural blink confirms it's really them, live.
Recorded Instantly
Attendance is saved the moment both checks pass — no manual entry.
Track on Dashboard
Both teacher and student see live counts, percentages, and eligibility status.
For Students

Check in with a glance.

Set up once, then mark attendance in seconds for every lecture — no app to install, just your browser.

  • 1
    Create your account
    Sign up with your name, roll number, and department.
  • 2
    Enroll your face
    A one-time camera setup — turn your head slowly for about 5 seconds.
  • 3
    Open your teacher's link
    Shared in your class group at the start of each lecture.
  • 4
    Scan and blink naturally
    The system checks your face and confirms you're really there.
  • 5
    Check your dashboard
    See your attendance percentage and eligibility status anytime.
Rules for Students
  • Your account and face enrollment are yours alone — never check in for someone else.
  • Keep your face clearly visible during scans: no mask, sunglasses, or photos held up to the camera.
  • A live blink is required every time — still images won't be accepted.
  • You're responsible for enrolling in the correct courses from your dashboard.
For Teachers

Run attendance without the register.

Start a lecture, share one link, and watch attendance fill in live — no headcounts, no sign-in sheets.

  • 1
    Create your faculty account
    Sign up with your name, email, and department.
  • 2
    Add your courses
    From your dashboard, add each course you teach with its code.
  • 3
    Start a lecture
    One click generates a unique check-in link for that session.
  • 4
    Share the link
    Post it in your class group or display it for students to open.
  • 5
    Finalize at the end
    One click checks everyone's running percentage and flags anyone below the threshold.
Rules for Teachers
  • Start the lecture link only when the class actually begins, and close it once attendance is taken.
  • Finalize attendance after every lecture so eligibility stays accurate for your students.
  • Review flagged students periodically — they're notified automatically, but a conversation still helps.
  • Each course you create is visible only on your own dashboard.
Policy

Rules & Regulations

The attendance policy applied across every course, calculated automatically from your check-in history.

Eligible
75% and above
Attendance meets the requirement. No action needed.
Condonation
65% – 74.9%
Below the requirement, in the provisional zone. Improve attendance promptly.
Not Eligible
Below 65%
Not eligible for the final exam in that course under current policy.
Conduct & Integrity
  • Attendance must be marked in person, by the enrolled student, at the time of the lecture.
  • Attempting to mark attendance using another person's photo, video, or account is a violation of academic integrity.
  • The liveness (blink) check exists specifically to prevent the above — still photos and screen replays are rejected.
  • Each student enrolls one face profile; re-enrollment should only happen for genuine setup issues.
Technical Requirements
  • A device with a working camera and a stable internet or campus network connection.
  • Good, even lighting during enrollment and check-in for reliable face matching.
  • Camera permission must be allowed in the browser when prompted.
